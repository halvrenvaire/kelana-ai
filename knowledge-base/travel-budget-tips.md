## How to Calculate Your Daily Travel Budget

### Formula
**Daily Budget = Total Budget ÷ Number of Days**

Allocate budget unevenly:
- First and last day: +20% (airport transfers, arrival meals)
- Activity-heavy days: +30%
- Rest/transit days: -20%

## Where to Save Money

### Accommodation (40–50% of budget)
- Book 2–3 months in advance for best rates
- Hostels: best for solo backpackers, $10–$25/night
- Airbnb: better value for groups (3+ people sharing)
- Avoid booking near major tourist sites — walk 10 minutes and save 30–50%

### Food (20–30% of budget)
- Eat where locals eat — avoid tourist menus
- Lunch is usually cheaper than dinner at the same restaurant
- Street food and market food is often better quality than tourist restaurants
- Grocery stores for breakfast saves $10–$20/day

### Transport (10–15% of budget)
- Book flights 6–8 weeks in advance
- Use public transport — often faster than taxis in traffic
- Overnight trains/buses save one night's accommodation
- Airport shuttle vs taxi: shuttle saves $10–$30 per trip

### Activities (15–20% of budget)
- Many world-class museums are free or have free days
- City tourism cards often include unlimited transport + museum entry
- Free walking tours exist in most major cities (tip-based)
- Book tours directly with local guides, not through hotels

## Money & Payment Tips

### ATMs vs Currency Exchange
- ATMs generally give better rates than airport exchange counters
- Withdraw larger amounts to minimize per-transaction fees
- Notify your bank before travel to avoid card blocks
- Carry some USD/EUR as emergency backup currency

### Cash vs Card by Region
- **Southeast Asia (Bali, Bangkok):** Cash preferred at markets and street food
- **Japan:** Still largely cash-based — always carry yen
- **Europe:** Cards widely accepted, carry some cash for small vendors
- **Turkey:** Mix — cash at bazaars, cards at restaurants

## Travel Insurance
- **Essential, not optional**
- Medical evacuation alone can cost $50,000–$200,000 without insurance
- Recommended coverage: medical ($100k+), trip cancellation, baggage loss
- Cost: $5–$15/day depending on coverage and destination

## Seasonal Pricing Strategy
- Shoulder season = same weather, 30–50% cheaper than peak
- Mid-week flights are typically 20–30% cheaper than weekends
- Holiday periods (Christmas, Golden Week, Eid) = 2–3x normal prices
