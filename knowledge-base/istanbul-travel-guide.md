# Istanbul, Turkey — Complete Travel Guide

## Overview
Istanbul straddles Europe and Asia across the Bosphorus Strait. It served as capital of both the Byzantine and Ottoman empires, offering a unique blend of Eastern and Western cultures, ancient mosques, vibrant bazaars, and stunning waterfront dining.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Spring (April–May):** Mild weather, tulip festival in April, fewer crowds.
- **Autumn (September–October):** Comfortable temperatures, less rain.
- **Summer (June–August):** Peak season, hot and crowded but vibrant.
- **Winter (December–February):** Fewer tourists, cheaper prices.

## Top Areas

### Sultanahmet (Old City)
- Home to Hagia Sophia, Blue Mosque, Topkapi Palace, Grand Bazaar
- Average accommodation: $40–$120/night (mid-range)

### Beyoglu / Taksim
- Famous for Istiklal Avenue, Galata Tower, rooftop bars
- Average accommodation: $35–$100/night

### Kadikoy (Asian Side)
- Trendy local neighborhood, food markets, street art
- Reached by ferry from Eminonu (15 min, $0.50)

## Budget Breakdown

### Budget Travel (USD/day)
- Accommodation: $15–$40
- Food: $10–$20 (street food, simit, lokanta)
- Transport: $3–$8 (Istanbulkart)
- **Total: ~$38–$88/day**

### Mid-Range Travel (USD/day)
- Accommodation: $50–$120
- Food: $25–$60
- Transport: $10–$20
- **Total: ~$105–$250/day**

### Luxury Travel (USD/day)
- Accommodation: $150–$500+ (Bosphorus view hotel)
- Food: $80–$200
- Transport: $30–$80
- **Total: ~$310–$880/day**

## Must-See Attractions
- **Hagia Sophia:** $15 full museum entry
- **Blue Mosque:** Free entry, closed during prayer times
- **Topkapi Palace:** $20 entry, allow 3–4 hours
- **Grand Bazaar:** 4,000+ shops, free entry, Mon–Sat 9am–7pm
- **Bosphorus Cruise:** Public ferry $2–$4; private tour $15–$30
- **Galata Tower:** $10 entry, panoramic views

## Must-Try Food
- **Simit:** Sesame bread ring, $0.30 from street carts
- **Döner Kebab:** $3–$6
- **Balık Ekmek:** Fish sandwich from boats at Eminonu, $3–$4
- **Baklava:** $5–$15/kg from Karakoy Gulluoglu
- **Turkish Tea (Çay):** $0.30–$1 everywhere
- **Turkish Coffee:** $2–$4, served with Turkish delight

## Visa Information
- **e-Visa:** Apply online at evisa.gov.tr. $50–$100 depending on nationality.
- Valid 180 days, multiple entry.

## Cultural Tips
- Dress modestly at mosques (cover shoulders and knees, women cover hair)
- Haggling expected at Grand Bazaar — start at 50% of asking price
- Remove shoes when entering mosques
- Tap water not recommended — buy bottled water
