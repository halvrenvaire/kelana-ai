# Bali, Indonesia — Complete Travel Guide

## Overview
Bali is a Indonesian island known for its forested volcanic mountains, iconic rice paddies, beaches, and coral reefs. The island is home to religious sites such as cliffside Uluwatu Temple.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Dry Season (April–October):** Best time to visit. Clear skies, perfect for beach activities and trekking.
- **Wet Season (November–March):** Cheaper accommodation, lush green rice terraces, fewer tourists.
- **Peak Season:** July–August and Christmas/New Year. Expect higher prices and crowds.

## Top Destinations

### Ubud
- Cultural heart of Bali
- Famous for Monkey Forest, Tegallalang Rice Terraces, and art galleries
- Best for: cultural immersion, yoga retreats, cooking classes
- Average accommodation: $30–$80/night (mid-range), $100–$300/night (luxury)

### Seminyak
- Upscale beach resort area
- Famous for sunset beach clubs (Ku De Ta, Potato Head)
- Best for: nightlife, fine dining, shopping
- Average accommodation: $50–$150/night (mid-range), $200–$500/night (luxury)

### Canggu
- Trendy surf town popular with digital nomads
- Famous for Echo Beach, rice field cafes, surf schools
- Best for: surfing, café hopping, budget travel
- Average accommodation: $20–$60/night (budget), $80–$200/night (mid-range)

### Uluwatu
- Clifftop temple and world-class surf breaks
- Famous for Uluwatu Temple, Kecak Fire Dance, Padang Padang Beach
- Best for: surfing, sunsets, romantic getaways
- Average accommodation: $40–$120/night (mid-range)

### Nusa Penida
- Remote island off Bali's southeast coast
- Famous for Kelingking Beach, Angel's Billabong, Broken Beach
- Best for: Instagram spots, snorkeling, manta ray diving
- Day trip cost: $30–$50 including boat and driver

## Budget Breakdown

### Budget Travel (USD/day)
- Accommodation: $10–$25 (guesthouse/hostel)
- Food: $10–$20 (warungs, local restaurants)
- Transport: $5–$15 (scooter rental $5–$8/day)
- Activities: $10–$20
- **Total: ~$35–$80/day**

### Mid-Range Travel (USD/day)
- Accommodation: $40–$100 (boutique hotel/villa)
- Food: $25–$50 (mix of local and tourist restaurants)
- Transport: $20–$40 (private driver)
- Activities: $20–$50
- **Total: ~$105–$240/day**

### Luxury Travel (USD/day)
- Accommodation: $150–$500+ (5-star resort/private villa)
- Food: $80–$200 (fine dining)
- Transport: $60–$100 (private car with driver)
- Activities: $50–$150
- **Total: ~$340–$950/day**

## Getting Around
- **Scooter/Motorbike:** Most popular, $5–$8/day rental. Requires international driving license.
- **Private Driver:** $35–$60/day for full-day hire. Best for day trips.
- **Grab/Gojek:** App-based transport, cheap in urban areas.
- **Blue Bird Taxi:** Metered taxi, reliable for airport transfers.

## Must-Try Food
- **Nasi Goreng:** Indonesian fried rice, $1–$3 at warungs
- **Babi Guling:** Suckling pig, famous in Ubud ($5–$10)
- **Sate Lilit:** Balinese minced meat satay
- **Lawar:** Traditional Balinese mixed dish
- **Fresh Coconut:** $1–$2 everywhere

## Visa Information
- **Visa on Arrival (VoA):** Available for 60+ nationalities, $35 USD, valid 30 days, extendable once
- **Visa-Free:** Several countries including ASEAN members
- Always check latest regulations before travel

## Health & Safety
- Drink bottled water only
- Use mosquito repellent (dengue risk)
- Travel insurance strongly recommended
- Bali Belly (traveler's diarrhea) is common — be careful with street food hygiene

## Cultural Tips
- Dress modestly when visiting temples (sarong required, usually provided)
- Remove shoes before entering temples and homes
- Use right hand for giving/receiving
- Avoid pointing feet at people or sacred objects
- Respect ceremony processions — step aside and do not interrupt
